Your wallet is personal, so the order of your tools should be too. If the one you reach for
every day is buried near the bottom, a few seconds fixes that.

Press and hold any card in your wallet. That opens **Reorder Cards**, a simple list of all
your tools. Use the up and down arrows next to each one to move it, nudging your favorites
toward the top and the ones you rarely touch further down. When it looks right, tap
**Done** to save the new order.

That's it. The next time you open the app, the tools that matter most are right where you
want them, ready the moment you need them.
