Most tools ask you to already know what you're looking for. Mental Health Wallet can work
the other way around: start from how you feel, and let it point you to something that fits.

On your wallet, tap the **Start from how I feel** card. Pick the emotion that matches where
you are right now. The app suggests tools that tend to help with that state, so you spend
your energy on the practice, not on searching.

Not sure what you're feeling? That happens. Tap the guided check-in instead. It asks a few
simple questions about your body, your mind, and your situation, and lands on a feeling
with you. From there you get the same tailored suggestions.

It takes under a minute, and there's no wrong answer. Next time you open the app and feel a
little off but can't name it, this is a good place to start.
