Thanks for signing up. Glad to have you.

Here's the deal with these emails: every now and then I'll send a short, practical tip to
help you get more out of the app, and, if you asked for them, the occasional gentle reminder
to come back. No spam, no pressure. You can change what you receive or stop anytime from the
link at the bottom of any email.

If you want one small thing to try right now: open the app and tap a card to use a tool.
That's the whole idea, a small toolkit of things that help, ready when you need them.

That's it for now. More soon, and only when it's actually useful.
