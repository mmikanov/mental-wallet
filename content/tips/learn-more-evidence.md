A coping tool is easier to use when you understand why it's supposed to help. So the tools
that come with Mental Health Wallet don't just tell you what to do, they show you the
reasoning.

Open any library tool and look for the **Learn more** link in its description. Tap it and
you'll see a plain-language explanation: how the technique works, the level of evidence
behind it, and a short summary of what research shows. For the ones where it makes sense,
there are further reading links so you can go deeper.

It's expert-sourced, not marketing. The idea is simple: you should be able to answer "why
should I trust this?" before you rely on it.

Next time a tool catches your eye, tap Learn more before you use it. A little context makes
the practice land better.
