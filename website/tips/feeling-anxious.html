Feeling anxious right now? Try this

When anxiety hits, the last thing you want is to scroll around deciding what might help. So let the app do that part.

Open Mental Health Wallet and tap Start from how I feel. Choose anxious. It'll suggest tools that tend to help with that state, often a short grounding or breathing exercise you can do right where you are. Pick one and follow it for a minute.

Not even sure "anxious" is the word? Use the guided check-in and it'll help you land on what you're feeling first, then suggest from there.

You don't have to ride it out alone or white-knuckle through it. A minute with the right tool can take the edge off. Keep this one in your back pocket for the next time it creeps up.
