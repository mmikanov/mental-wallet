Can't find your app? Add it yourself

The library has cards for a lot of popular apps, but it can't cover everything. If the app you rely on isn't there, you don't have to go without. You can add it yourself.

Open the menu (the ⋮ at the top right of your wallet) and choose Create Tool. Give your card a name and an icon in the first step. In the next step, tap Add block and pick Link Button. That's the piece that turns your card into a launcher.

Fill in three things: a Label for the button (like "Open my app"), a Target URL, and an optional Fallback URL. For the target, use the app's website (starting with https://), or its link if you know it. Set the fallback to somewhere useful, like the app's website, so you still land somewhere if the app isn't installed. Then preview and save.

Now that card lives in your wallet alongside everything else, and tapping its button opens your app. Your whole toolkit stays in one place, even the parts we didn't think of.
