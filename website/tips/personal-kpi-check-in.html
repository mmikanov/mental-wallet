Track the thing that matters to you

"Getting better" means different things to different people. For one person it's managing stress; for another it's sleeping better, or feeling more present. Mental Health Wallet lets you define it on your own terms.

During setup you pick what success means to you. From then on, the daily check-in, the seedling button on your wallet, lets you log how you're tracking against that personal measure. Not a one-size-fits-all wellness score, but the thing you actually care about.

Over time those check-ins become a simple, honest record of your own progress. Some days up, some days down, and that's fine. The point is to see the trend in your own words.

If you set a personal measure during setup, try tapping the seedling today and logging where you're at. It only takes a moment.
