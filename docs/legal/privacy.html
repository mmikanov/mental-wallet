Mental Health Wallet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Data Controller

Mental Health Wallet is the data controller for information processed through this app. If you have questions about how your data is handled, contact us at privacy@mentalhealthwallet.app.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only anonymous usage events to understand how the app is used. These include:

  • App open events
  • Session timing
  • Tool opens and completions
  • Outcome responses
  • Start mode selections

Each device is assigned a random UUID that cannot be linked to a person.

We do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • GPS or precise location data
  • Journal content or personal entries
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact lists

3. Purpose of Data Collection

We use anonymous analytics exclusively for product improvement:

  • Understanding which tools help users most
  • Improving the onboarding experience
  • Measuring feature adoption
  • Identifying areas needing better support

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond improving the app.

4. Data Retention

Anonymous usage events are retained for a maximum of 24 months, after which they are permanently deleted.

Events stored locally on your device are transmitted to our servers and then removed from the device. You can delete all local data at any time via the Settings screen.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) that are bound by data processing agreements. No personally identifiable information is shared because none is collected.

6. Your Rights

  • Opt out: Toggle off analytics in the app's Settings screen.
  • Data reset: Use "Reset my app data" in Settings to clear all local data.
  • Deletion: Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to delete.
  • Access: Anonymous data cannot be attributed to any individual, so access requests cannot be fulfilled.

7. Children's Privacy

Mental Health Wallet is not directed at children under 13. Users between 13 and 18 years old should review this policy with a parent or guardian before using the app.

8. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All network communication uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Data is encrypted at rest on our servers
  • Your anonymous device ID is stored in the secure keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android)
  • We follow the minimal data principle — we only collect what is needed

9. Policy Updates

When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will provide notice within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@mentalhealthwallet.app.
